Portal Services

Core Operational Modules

Each service module is built to keep polar research stations, field traverses, equipment maintenance, and supply logistics structured and verifiable.

Expedition Management

Plan, track, and update missions across Planned, Active, Completed, and Cancelled phases with unified expedition dossiers linking crew, assets, tasks, and shipments.

Personnel Roster & Roles

Maintain directories of Expedition Leaders, Researchers, Engineers, Logistics Coordinators, Medical Officers, and Technicians with emergency satellite contact records.

Asset & Fleet Registry

Catalog snowmobiles, generators, satellite phones, GPS devices, and heating systems with automatic overdue and 14-day upcoming maintenance indicators.

Supplies & Stock Alerts

Monitor food rations, fuel reserves, medical kits, water, and batteries across depots with automatic low-stock alerts when quantities hit minimum safety thresholds.

Multi-Modal Logistics

Coordinate Ski-Plane Air Drops, Twin Otter Cargo Flights, Icebreaker Vessels, Snowcat Overland Convoys, Helicopter Slings, and Snowmobile Sleds from dispatch to delivery.

Maintenance & Task Control

Schedule cold-weather overhauls, pre-departure inspections, and priority mission tasks with one-click status updates and persistent audit logs.

Operational Standard

Three-Phase Expedition Workflow

A structured administrative lifecycle for high-latitude operations from station preparation to mission debriefing.

Phase 01

Plan & Register

Register polar stations with coordinates, establish expedition timelines and objectives, and assign qualified personnel roles.

Phase 02

Inspect & Dispatch

Verify pre-departure equipment maintenance status, confirm supply reserves meet minimum thresholds, and schedule multi-modal cargo shipments.

Phase 03

Monitor & Audit

Track active deployments, priority tasks, low-stock warnings, and equipment service alerts in real time through the Operations Dashboard.

About PolarOps

Built for Transparency, Safety, and Institutional Reliability

Operating across Arctic and Antarctic stations demands complete accountability. Fragmented spreadsheets increase operational risk when weather windows close or critical equipment requires servicing.

PolarOps combines Django 6.1 server-side security with MongoDB Atlas document persistence to deliver a responsive, accessible portal for managing polar operations end-to-end.
